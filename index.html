SMALL STEPS, YOUR WAY

Everyday confidence

There’s no single right way to stay safe. Choose ideas that feel useful for you.

01

Share your plan

Let someone you trust know where you’re going and when you expect to arrive.

↗
02

Keep your phone ready

Charge it before you leave, and save important contacts where you can reach them quickly.

⌁
03

Trust your instincts

If a situation feels uncomfortable, it’s okay to leave, ask for help, or change your plan.

✳
04

Look out for one another

Check in with friends, and ask if someone nearby would like support.

♡
♡

SUPPORT IS HERE

You don’t have to figure it out alone.

If you’re in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or someone you trust. For ongoing support, look for a women’s support organisation in your area.
